### Steps to Contribute

Step 0: Get a  _**temporary link and password**_ that are exclusively assigned to you from the admin.

Example:

_🔗 Link: https://notes-aid.minavkaria.in/edit/abcdefghijklmno_

_🔒 Password: ****_


_Step 1: Login with Google_

_Step 2: Open the link sent to you be the admin. Enter the password given to you._

<img width="1347" height="994" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/028bf5b7-afac-4305-aa3c-335ec0a122fd" />

Step 3: Start Contributing, here you create a new module, notes/resource, topics, videos

eg: 

Subject - AM1

- Module 1
  - Resource and Notes
    - Notes Title and Drive links
  - Topics 
    - Module 1.1
      - Video titles and Youtube Links 
    - Module 1.2
      - Video titles and Youtube Links
    - .... so on
- Module 2
  - Resource and Notes
     - Notes Title and Drive link
   - Topics 
     - Module 2.1
       - Video titles and Youtube Links
     - Module 2.2
       - Video titles and Youtube Links
- ... so on

The Textboxes are arranged in this order, so you can add them accordingly

- Show Preview - You can preview your work before "Submit for preview" 
- Logout - Logout the session after your work is done
- No Changes to Submit/Submit Review - Submit your work for review to the admin.
- Your changes are saved locally, your work won't be deleted even if you logout, or turn off the browser.

### Others Important instructions ✅✅:

- Submit for Review only once.
- Get your submission approved and merged in the website, after that only continue with your further work. ( Or else this could cause Race Condition, this issue will be fixed in the future).
